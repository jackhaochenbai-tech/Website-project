PHILLY 76ERS

Even in the final stages of his historic career, LeBron James remains one of the NBA's most elite performers—especially when the postseason arrives. His recent signing with the Philadelphia 76ers proved that "The King" still commands the basketball world. Within just 48 hours, James' debut Sixers jersey shattered records, completely selling out and eclipsing Shohei Ohtani’s historic 2023 Dodgers debut sales. Even in a modern, digital era, fans rushed for tangible history, pushing his Topps NOW card sales past the 200,000 mark. The true testament to his unmatched drawing power, however, lies in the broadcast schedules: following his arrival, the Sixers' nationally televised games skyrocketed from a mere 14 last season to the league maximum of 34 national broadcasts for the 2026–27 season.

The NBA's all-time leading scorer moved back East on a veteran minimum deal to chase one final championship journey. At 41, he brings unparalleled basketball IQ, flawless elite passing, and championship pedigree to the frontcourt.

JOEL EMBIID

27 ppg, 11 reb 4ast
The definitive focal point of the franchise. An unstoppable former NBA MVP, Embiid is a dominant force in the high post who commands double-teams every single night. When healthy, he is one of the most efficient and physically imposing players on the planet.

JAYLEN BROWN

20 ppg, 6 reb 3 ast
Acquired via a blockbuster trade from the Boston Celtics, Brown is a former Finals MVP and dynamic All-NBA wing. He provides the team with fierce perimeter defense, explosive driving ability, and postseason toughness.

TYRESE MAXEY

21 ppg 3 reb 5 ast
The absolute engine and heart of the team's pace. Fresh off career-best years, Maxey is a lightning-fast, high-scoring All-NBA guard who stretches defenses with his deep shooting range and relentless energy.

VJ EDGECOMB

16 ppg 6 reb 3 ast
The youth and athletic spark plug. Taken as the No. 3 overall draft pick, this high-flying rookie balances out the veterans with incredible raw athleticism, cutting ability, and elite defensive upside.
